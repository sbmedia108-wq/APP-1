## 12. Tester ask (Google Play closed test)

Google Play requires new personal developer accounts to run a closed test with **at least 12 testers who stay opted in for 14 days in a row** before the app can go public ([Google Play Console Help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465)). For an early-January launch, the test needs to **start by early December**. Aim for 15–20 testers so you stay above 12 if anyone drops out. Testers need an Android phone and the Google account email they use for the Play Store.
