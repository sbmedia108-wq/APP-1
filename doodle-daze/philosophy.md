# Kerf & Kindness

**Philosophy:** Warmth made by precision. The line a laser leaves in birch ply — the *kerf* — is a mark of absolute exactness that nevertheless produces the softest, most domestic objects: a star on a ribbon, a tag on a parcel. This movement lives in that paradox. Every form is cut cleanly; every feeling is handmade.

**Space and form.** Shapes are positive and negative at once: a disc of wood defined by the star that is missing from it. Compositions are built from a small vocabulary of perfect geometry — circles, five-point stars, stacked triangles, six-fold snowflakes — repeated with the patience of a workshop batch run. Generous cream space surrounds every element so that each cut reads like an object laid on paper, not a sticker on a crowded page. The work should look meticulously crafted, as though each piece was positioned by hand and re-measured many times.

**Colour and material.** A restrained palette drawn from the bench: unbleached paper cream, raw birch, scorched-edge umber, a deep pine green, a softer sage, and a single berry red used as rarely as a wax seal. Texture is felt rather than shown — a faint paper grain, a slight burn at an edge. Nothing glossy, nothing digital-bright. Colour is calibrated with painstaking attention so that the whole reads as one material family.

**Scale and rhythm.** One voice speaks loudly, everything else whispers. A single flowing italic name carries the piece; numerals are set large and monumental like stamped markings; supporting words are small, letterspaced, almost clinical — workshop labels on a drawer. Rhythm comes from repetition: a row of hanging forms, a border of tiny motifs, intervals measured exactly.

**Composition and hierarchy.** Symmetry anchored on a strong central axis, as calm as a mantelpiece, broken only once by a human gesture — a handwritten aside, slightly tilted, as if added in pencil after the printing. That one imperfection proves the rest is deliberate. The finished piece must feel like the product of deep expertise and master-level execution: every alignment checked, nothing crowding, nothing falling off the edge, every margin breathing.
