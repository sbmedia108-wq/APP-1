How to deliver it

One CV in under an hour, using Claude.

Claude makes each CV fast to write, which is how the prices stay low. You add the judgement and checking.

Collect the facts

The Google Form asks for: target job, every job with dates, what they actually did, any numbers (sales, customers, team size), qualifications, skills, and an old CV if they have one. Also ask for 1–2 job adverts they want to apply for.

Draft with Claude

Paste the form answers and the job advert into Claude with a prompt like this:

Write a UK-style 2-page CV for this person, targeting the job advert below. Use ONLY facts from their answers; never invent jobs, dates, grades or numbers. Turn duties into achievements, using their real numbers where given. Include: profile (3–4 lines), key skills matching the advert's keywords, experience (newest first, 3–5 bullet points each), education, extras. Plain formatting that applicant tracking systems can read. No photo, age or marital status. If something useful is missing, list questions I should ask them. [form answers] [job advert]

Edit & check it yourself

Read every line. Ask the customer the questions Claude flagged. Put the text into your Canva or Google Docs CV template and export Word + PDF.

Deliver, then follow up

Send the files with a short note on how to tailor it for each job. Two days later, send the review request (page 6).

Quality checklist before sending

  • Every fact matches what the customer told you
  • No spelling errors (read it aloud)
  • Contact details correct & professional email
  • Dates consistent, newest job first
  • Keywords from the job advert included
  • Fits on 1 page (Starter) or 2 pages (Pro)
  • No photo, age, date of birth or marital status
  • Customer's data deleted after 3 months, and they've been told
