The Hampton CV mark.

A page with a folded corner (a CV) carrying an H (Hampton). It's simple enough to read as a 16px browser icon and bold enough for a shop sign.

Do

  • Leave clear space around it at least the width of the H
  • Use the reversed version on dark photos or forest green
  • Use the one-colour versions for stamps, embroidery and photocopies

Don't

  • Stretch, rotate or add shadows
  • Recolour the page anything but amber, black or white
  • Retype the name in another font (use the files)
