Is Mavis a real person?

No. Mavis is the app's name. She works from guides we wrote and checked, and never asks for bank details.

How much does the app cost?

Free to download, with every guide. A Plus plan is planned at £3.99 a month.

Does the app work on an iPhone?

Not yet. Android phones and tablets come first, iPhone after.

Does Mavis need the internet?

The guides work without it. Talking to Mavis works best on Wi-Fi or mobile data.
