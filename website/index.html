Questions

Is Mavis a real person?

No. Mavis is a friendly computer helper with a familiar name. She'll never pretend to be a person, and she'll never ask for your passwords, bank details or codes.

How much will it cost?

Ask Mavis is free to download, and all the built-in guides and scam warnings are free for good. Ask Mavis Plus, for unlimited "ask anything" help, will be £3.99 a month or £34.99 a year.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not at first. Ask Mavis is starting on Android phones and tablets. iPhone will follow, so join the waitlist and we'll tell you when.

What do you do with my email?

We only use it to tell you about Ask Mavis, and you can leave the list at any time. We never sell it or share it. See our privacy notice.
