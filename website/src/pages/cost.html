Risk management

Find it, size it, deal with it.

Identify

We review design, construction and maintenance to find risks to cost, programme, compliance, quality and safety.

Assess

Risk matrices rank each risk by likelihood and impact, so attention goes where it matters.

Mitigate

Reduce, transfer, accept or avoid, with a clear owner and plan for each.

Dispute resolution

A commercial settlement, without escalation.

We act as peacemaker between parties, weigh the merits of each grievance and seek agreement quickly and comprehensively. We also advise on recovering retention held without justification.

  • Claims for loss and expense, extensions of time, disruption and acceleration
  • Valuation of instructions, certificates and variations
  • Insurance claims preparation and assessment
  • Valuations for contract determination, insolvency or receivership
  • Advice and support to resolve disputes effectively
