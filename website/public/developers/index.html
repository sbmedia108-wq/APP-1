Services we provide

Three stages. One team throughout.

Stage 01 · Project inception

A clear, deliverable business plan.

We assess opportunities with a strategic eye, balancing cost, risk, opportunity and value. Understanding site constraints and market dynamics lets us shape a plan that guides decisions from the outset.

  • Searching out and finding prospective sites
  • Preparing feasibility studies
  • Comparing private and public sector funding options
  • Negotiating development agreements
  • Appointing and managing the professional team
Stage 02 · Construction period

One vision, carried through the whole team.

We define a clear vision with you, then communicate and deliver it through the professional team we appoint. Strong relationships with local authorities and stakeholders help secure the right planning and development consents.

  • Setting out the project brief
  • Managing the design and procurement process
  • Managing the construction team
  • Managing the construction process
Stage 03 · Project completion

A smooth handover, and support after it.

We lead main contractor procurement through strong relationships across the contractor and supply chain markets, then oversee delivery with a constant focus on cost, quality and programme.

  • Managing handover to the end user
  • Facilities management of the completed building
  • End-user support for the client after completion
