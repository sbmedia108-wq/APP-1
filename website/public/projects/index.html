Property defect resolution & repairs

Defects found, diagnosed and fixed.

Whether you've just moved into a new build or are dealing with long-standing problems, we identify, diagnose and resolve issues efficiently and professionally.

  • New build defect resolution with developers and homeowners
  • Snagging inspections with a report for your developer
  • Structural repairs: subsidence, cracking and foundations
  • Damp, condensation and water ingress
  • Roofing and external envelope repairs
  • Internal finishing: plastering, painting, flooring and joinery
