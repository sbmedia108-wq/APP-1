# The Boundary Boys – ad briefs

Six new ads plus one corrected re-issue. Draft graphics for each are in `graphics/exports/`.

## House style (applies to every ad)

- **Formula:** pun headline + the two lads + slanted yellow tape with the phone number.
- **Colours:** hazard yellow `#FFD84A`, black, white. One accent colour per ad at most.
- **Type:** heavy condensed capitals (Anton for headlines, Oswald for supporting lines).
- **Always on the ad:** "The Boundary Boys", 07542 666563, and one reason to call (free quote, storm repairs, area).
- **Voice:** cheeky, local, plain-spoken. The joke should carry a selling point, not just be a joke.
- **Avoid:** film characters, film logos and real police insignia. Parody the idea, not the artwork.

> **Check before publishing:** the drafts say "free quotes", "concreted in" and "Southampton". Change these if they don't match what you actually offer.
