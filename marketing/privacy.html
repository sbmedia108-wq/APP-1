The short version

Your quotes, clients and settings are stored on your phone. We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your business details, rates and prices.
  • Quotes, including client names, phone numbers, addresses and site photos you add.
  • Your tool list and who you've lent tools to.

This stays on your device unless you choose to share it (for example by sending a quote on WhatsApp or saving a backup file).

Voice

When you tap the microphone, your phone's built-in speech recognition turns speech into text. On Android this is provided by Google and on iPhone by Apple, under their own privacy policies. Graft does not record or keep audio.

AI quote drafting

When you're online and tap "Build my quote", the text of what you said, your rates, and summaries of up to three similar past jobs are sent to our server, which uses Anthropic's Claude to draft the quote. We don't use this to identify you, and it is not used to train AI models. [State how long your server keeps request logs, e.g. "We keep request logs for 30 days for troubleshooting."] If you're offline, quotes are drafted entirely on your phone.

Location

If you tap the location button in Materials, your location is used once to look up your postcode, so merchant links can point to branches near you. It isn't stored or sent to us.

Camera and photos

Used only when you add site photos to a quote. Photos are kept in the app's private storage on your phone.

Sharing you choose to do

Sending a quote opens WhatsApp or your share sheet; what happens next is covered by that app's policy. Merchant links open the merchant's website.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask what we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing [email]. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

Graft is for businesses and isn't intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes we'll update the date above and tell you in the app.
