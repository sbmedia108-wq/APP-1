The big idea

Every trade has said “I’ll send the price over tonight.” Graft makes it true before the van door shuts.

The enemy isn't a competitor app. It's the 9pm kitchen table: receipts, a calculator, a cold brew and a quote that should have gone out four hours ago. We sell the evening back.

Time

Quote on the drive

Talk for two minutes, send before you leave. The quote's done while the client's still waving you off.

Money

First price in, first to be booked

Homeowners ask three trades. The one who replies tonight looks keen. The one who replies Thursday looks busy.

Sanity

The boring bits, sorted

Receipts for the accountant, the kit in the van, the job in the calendar. Fewer “where's my drill” moments.

Tone of voice

Talk like the trade. Not like a bank.

Warm, quick, a bit cheeky. We laugh with tradespeople, never at them. If it wouldn't land in the merchant queue at 7am, rewrite it.

Say

  • “Got a job to price?”
  • “Done before the kettle's boiled.”
  • “Your prices, not some made-up average.”
  • “Nudge them on WhatsApp.”
  • Plain numbers: £12.99 a month, 14 days free.

Avoid

  • “Leverage AI-powered estimation workflows.”
  • “Streamline your business operations.”
  • Jokes about clients, cowboys or other trades.
  • Anything that sounds like a parking fine.
  • Claims we can't back up (“save 10 hours a week!”) until testers prove it.

01 · The Kitchen Table

Same job. 87 minutes earlier.

  1. 0–4s9:47pm. Kitchen table. Receipts everywhere, calculator, cold brew. Text: “Every tradesman at 9:47pm”.
  2. 4–8sHe rubs his eyes. Phone buzzes: “Any update on that price? 😊”
  3. 8–10sHard cut. Rewind sound. Daylight, client's drive, same lad.
  4. 10–22sTalks into Graft for real. Quote builds on screen. Taps Send on WhatsApp.
  5. 22–27sClient at the door, phone pings: “Brilliant, when can you start?”
  6. 27–30sHe gets in the van. Text: “Quote it on the drive. Home for tea.”

CTA: 14 days free · link in bio

02 · The Ballpark

Faster than “ballpark?”

  1. 0–3sHomeowner, arms folded: “Can you just give us a rough ballpark?”
  2. 3–5sTradesman, deadpan, to camera: “I can do better than a ballpark.”
  3. 5–17sWalks the garden talking into the phone: metres, posts, gravel boards, two days.
  4. 17–24sHomeowner's phone pings. Itemised quote on WhatsApp. Eyebrows up.
  5. 24–30sHomeowner: “...is that the actual price?” Tradesman: “That's the actual park.” Logo.

CTA: “Your prices. Not a guess.”

03 · The Drill

Don't be the lad who left the drill.

  1. 0–5s7am, site. Lad opens the van. Empty space where the drill should be. Long silence.
  2. 5–9sText: “Day 1 of the job.” Boss's face. Seagull noise.
  3. 9–20sRewind. Night before. Graft van check: tick, tick, tick, “All loaded. Off you go.”
  4. 20–26sMorning, van opens, drill there. He kisses it. (Optional. Funnier.)
  5. 26–30sText: “Graft. Quotes, receipts, and the kit you'd forget.”

CTA: “Free van check inside. No, really.”

The flow

From one phone to everyone’s

Five stages. Nobody moves to the next one until its gate is met. That's how we avoid launching to 5,000 people with a bug that 50 would have spotted.

STAGE 0

Get it ready

AI server live, accounts and backup, subscriptions, store listing, privacy policy.

GateEvery product and store item below is green

STAGE 1

Waitlist

Landing page with sign-up. Seed it from your own contacts, trade Facebook groups and merchant posters.

Gate300+ on the list, at least 8 different trades

STAGE 2

Founding 50

Hand-picked from the list. Google Play closed test. Weekly WhatsApp check-in. Fix what they moan about.

Gate12+ testers active for 14 days (Google's rule) and 30+ real quotes sent

STAGE 3

Launch week

Production release on Google Play. Waitlist gets the link first. Videos go out, ads switch on at £10 a day.

GateCrash-free rate above 99% in week one

STAGE 4

Grow

Referrals, merchant counters, trade groups. iPhone version once Android pays its way.

Gate100 paying, then build for iPhone
