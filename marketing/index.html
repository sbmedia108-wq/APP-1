How it works

Walk it. Talk it. Send it.

No forms, no spreadsheets, no evenings at the kitchen table. Say it the way you’d tell your labourer and Graft does the sums.

STEP 1 · TALK

Say what the job is

“Twenty metres of closeboard down the side, concrete posts and gravel boards, old fence to the tip, two and a half days with a labourer.”

~ 90 seconds, stood on the drive

STEP 2 · CHECK

Graft prices it up

Labour at your day rate, posts, rails, featheredge and postcrete counted with waste, your markup applied. It flags anything it had to guess.

~ 60 seconds to tweak a line or two

STEP 3 · SEND

One tap to WhatsApp

A clean, itemised quote lands in your client’s WhatsApp with the deposit to book in. PDF too, if they want one for the file.

~ 10 seconds. On to the next one.
