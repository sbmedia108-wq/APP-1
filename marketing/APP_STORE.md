## Description
Price it on the drive. Send it before the van door shuts.

Graft is the quoting app for UK tradespeople doing private work: fencers, landscapers, plasterers, decorators, joiners, tilers, bricklayers, roofers and handymen. Talk through the job for a couple of minutes and Graft writes the itemised quote, counts the materials and sends it to your client on WhatsApp.

**TALK, DON'T TYPE**
Say it like you'd say it to your labourer: "Twenty metres of closeboard, concrete posts and gravel boards, old fence to the tip, two and a half days with a labourer." Graft understands trade talk and turns it into labour, materials and waste lines at your rates.

**IT REMEMBERS YOUR JOBS**
Done something similar before? Graft shows you what you charged last time and lets you start from it. Your own prices always beat guide prices.

**SEND ON WHATSAPP**
One tap sends a clean, itemised quote with deposit and time on site. PDF quotes and invoices too.

**CHEAPEST MATERIALS NEAR YOU**
Say "twelve 4x4 eight-foot posts" and compare Screwfix, Toolstation, Travis Perkins, Jewson, Selco, Wickes and B&Q near your postcode.

**HOW MUCH DO I NEED?**
Calculators for panel and closeboard fencing, decking, patios, board & skim, paint and turf. Straight into a quote.

**VAN CHECK**
A tick-list per kit before you leave site, and a record of who's borrowed what.

**NEVER LET A QUOTE GO COLD**
Graft reminds you to chase quotes and writes the nudge for you.

Works with no signal. VAT, deposits and payment terms handled. Your data stays on your phone.

## Brand
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#0B1726` | Primary surface, text, headers |
| Ink 2 | `#14253A` | Gradients, raised dark surfaces |
| Hi-vis amber | `#FFB020` | The one action colour: primary buttons, mic, highlights |
| Amber deep | `#E89600` | Amber text on light backgrounds |
| Ground | `#F4F5F7` | App background |
| WhatsApp green | `#25D366` | Only on "Send on WhatsApp" |

Why: navy reads as a premium, trustworthy trade van livery; hi-vis amber is the colour of site safety, instantly familiar to the trade, and is kept for the single thing to tap next. The hazard-chevron band is the brand's signature graphic.

**Type:** Archivo (condensed, 800–900, uppercase) for headlines; Barlow for body; IBM Plex Mono for labels and figures. In-app uses the platform system font for speed and legibility.

**Voice:** Plain, direct, a bit of site banter. "Got a job to price?" not "Create a new estimate." Never patronising. Short sentences.
