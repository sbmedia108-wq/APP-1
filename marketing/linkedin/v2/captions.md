## 3. `03-tenders-line-up.png`

If your tenders don't line up, your BOQ didn't.

Spot the odd one out? When the bill of quantities is vague, every contractor prices something different, and you end up comparing apples with pears.

A properly measured BOQ gives every bidder the same items, the same quantities and the same rules, so the prices you get back are prices you can compare.

✔ Measured to NRM2 (or SMM7 when needed)
✔ BIM-based take-off
✔ Delivered in ConQuest, CITE, Excel or PDF
✔ Full audit trail, plus support through tender queries
