## 2. The cost of change (`02-cost-of-change.png`)

The cheapest time to change a design is before it's drawn.

Early in a project, at RIBA stages 0 to 2, you have the most influence over cost and changes cost very little. By the time you're on site, that flips: your ability to influence cost has fallen, and every change costs far more.

That's why we embed cost advice inside the design process. Working alongside architects, engineers and contractors, we test value engineering options while they're still cheap to make. That reduces abortive work, controls cost and protects quality.

The earlier we're involved, the more we can save. When does your QS usually join the team?

## 3. Bills of quantities (`03-bills-of-quantities.png`)

A good bill of quantities is the difference between tenders you can compare and tenders you can't.

Our BOQs itemise every material, part and labour element of your project, so contractors price accurately and your interests stay protected.

✔ Measured to NRM2, or SMM7 where your project calls for it
✔ Model-based take-off using BIM
✔ Delivered in ConQuest, CITE, Excel or PDF
✔ Clear audit trail through dimsheets and digitisation sheets

And we don't disappear at issue. We support tender queries and addendums, recommend value engineering options and carry out independent quantity checks.

Need a BOQ? Get a quote at eklipseconstructiongroup.com/cost
