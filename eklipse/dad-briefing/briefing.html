The big idea

We don't need to buy any equipment to start.

We run Eklipse as a booking agency. You find the clients, and we book local freelance DJs and live acts who bring their own kit and insurance. They play the event, and Eklipse keeps the difference.

How one booking makes money (example)

💷 Client pays Eklipse£850
🎧 Freelance DJ gets (brings their own kit)£550–£650
✅ Eklipse keeps£200–£300

Why start this way

  • No big spend before we know it works
  • We can take bookings straight away
  • We can cover more than one event a night

Later on

Once the business has made enough profit, you buy your own kit and start DJing gigs yourself again, keeping the full fee.

For reference, UK couples typically pay about £850–£1,300 for a wedding DJ, and the average entertainment booking around Coventry is about £795.

One important thing

We only say what's true.

The current website says things we can't back up yet. Misleading customers is against UK consumer law, and if someone books expecting a decade of experience, it could backfire badly.

Take out

  • "Over a decade of experience"
  • "Our DJs are seasoned professionals"
  • PR, crisis comms and audio branding (until we can show examples)
  • Any reviews we don't actually have

What we CAN say

  • "Behind the decks since the 90s"
  • "Hand-picked, vetted, insured DJs and live acts"
  • "One point of contact for the whole night"
  • Podcast production (my side)
It started behind the decks in the 90s. That's a great story.

📸 Please dig these out

Any old photos, flyers or mixtapes from your 90s DJ days. Real history beats stock photos every time, and they'll be some of our best posts.

The money

Start small. Grow from profit.

Step 1 · Now

£100–250

  • Insurance ~£60–150/yr
  • Cards + venue flyers ~£40–80
  • Website, Google, social: free

Step 2 · After 1st deposit

£500–900

  • Solicitor checks our contract
  • Small ads test
  • Photos at our first events

Step 3 · From profit only

£2–4k

  • Practice controller first
  • Then second-hand gig kit
  • So you can DJ yourself
The rule: we don't buy DJ kit until the business has paid for it.

A realistic first year (part-time)

Cautious: 6 bookings, no podcast client~£900
Good: 15 bookings + 1 podcast client for 6 months~£5,000
Strong: 25 bookings + 1 podcast client most of the year~£10,000

Profit after costs. These are estimates. It's a side business to start, and a strong year pays for your own kit.
