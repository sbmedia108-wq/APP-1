Where to look

The 15-minute call

What to ask, and what to check.

Questions to ask

  • "How long have you been playing weddings and parties? Roughly how many a year?"
  • "What kit do you bring? Speakers, lights, a mic for speeches? Do you have backup?"
  • "What's your fee for a 4-hour evening set? And per extra hour?"
  • "How far will you travel? Do you charge for it?"
  • "What do you do if a guest keeps requesting songs that don't fit?" (you want a calm, sensible answer)
  • "Which Saturdays are you free next year?"

Before you sign them, tick all of these

☐ Public liability insurance certificate (in date, at least £5m)
☐ PAT testing certificate for their kit (in date)
☐ Video of them playing a real event (not just a mix)
☐ At least 2 reviews or a reference you've called
☐ Photos you're allowed to use on our website and socials
☐ Turned up to the call on time and was easy to talk to
Red flags: no insurance, won't send a video, vague about kit, badmouths other clients, or late replying to you. If they're slow with you, they'll be slow with our clients.

Freelancer agreement: draft

Eklipse Media freelance performer agreement

Draft template, not legal advice. Have a solicitor check it before using it (it's in the Step 2 budget).

Between: Eklipse Media Limited ("Eklipse") and ______________________ ("the Performer"). Date: ___________

  1. Bookings. Eklipse may offer the Performer bookings. Each booking is confirmed in writing (email or WhatsApp) with the date, venue, times, fee and client requirements (the "Booking Sheet"). Neither party has to offer or accept any particular booking.
  2. Fee and payment. Eklipse pays the fee on the Booking Sheet by bank transfer within 7 days after the event, once the Performer sends an invoice. The client pays Eklipse, never the Performer directly.
  3. Equipment. The Performer provides professional, safe, PAT-tested equipment suitable for the venue, including backup for essential items (for example, a second music source and cables).
  4. Insurance. The Performer holds public liability insurance of at least £5 million (or more if the venue requires it) and gives Eklipse a current certificate.
  5. On the day. The Performer arrives at least 90 minutes before the start time (or as agreed), dresses smartly, follows the venue's rules (including sound limits and curfews), stays sober enough to work professionally, and treats the client, guests and venue staff with respect.
  6. Representing Eklipse. At Eklipse bookings the Performer represents Eklipse Media. They do not hand out their own business cards or promote their own services to the client or guests.
  7. Clients. For 12 months after an event, the Performer agrees not to take direct bookings from clients introduced by Eklipse. Any such enquiry goes through Eklipse.

  1. Cancellation by the Performer. The Performer gives at least 4 weeks' notice if they can't do a confirmed booking, except in a genuine emergency, and helps Eklipse find suitable cover where possible.
  2. Cancellation by the client. If a client cancels a confirmed booking, Eklipse pays the Performer ______% of their fee when the cancellation is within ______ days of the event. Otherwise no fee is due.
  3. Photos and video. Eklipse may use photos and video of the Performer at Eklipse events, and material the Performer supplies, to promote Eklipse, with credit where practical.
  4. Client information. The Performer uses client and guest details only for the booking, keeps them private and deletes them afterwards, in line with UK data-protection law.
  5. Self-employed. The Performer is an independent contractor, not an employee, and is responsible for their own tax and National Insurance.
  6. Ending this agreement. Either party may end this agreement with 4 weeks' written notice. Confirmed bookings still go ahead unless both parties agree otherwise.
  7. Law. This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales.
