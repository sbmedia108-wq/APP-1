FAQ

Good questions.

Do you travel outside Warwickshire?

Yes. We work across the UK. Travel within the Midlands is usually included, and for anywhere further we'll quote it upfront.

Do you bring all the equipment?

Yes. Full technical set-up is included with every DJ and live music booking: sound system, DJ equipment and basic lighting. Bigger staging and lighting rigs can be added.

How far ahead should I book?

For Saturdays from May to September and for December party season, 9–12 months ahead is safest. For weekday corporate events, a few weeks is often enough, so just ask.

Can you cover a wedding ceremony, drinks and the evening party?

Yes. Many couples book an acoustic act for the ceremony and drinks, then a DJ for the evening. We coordinate both so the changeover is seamless.

Can you record a podcast remotely?

Yes. We produce remote and studio recordings and handle editing, mastering and publishing to Spotify, Apple Podcasts and YouTube.
