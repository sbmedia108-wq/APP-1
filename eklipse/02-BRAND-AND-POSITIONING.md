# 02. Brand and positioning

## The core decision

Eklipse Media currently tries to be a DJ company, an event producer, a PR agency, a podcast studio and a sonic-branding house all at once. That's too many front doors. Two structural options:

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| A. Split into two brands | Very clear | Twice the marketing on a small budget |
| **B. One brand, two divisions (recommended)** | One reputation, cross-selling, simple | Needs discipline on the homepage |

**Recommendation: one brand, two divisions.**

- **Eklipse Live** covers DJs, live music and event production. *This is the cash engine and where 10+ years of proof sits. It leads the homepage.*
- **Eklipse Studio** covers podcast production, audio branding, PR and marketing. *This is the growth engine: recurring revenue that isn't tied to weekends.*

The link between them is **sound**. "We set the tone" works literally (audio) and figuratively (atmosphere, reputation).

## Positioning statement

> For couples, companies and venues in Warwickshire and the Midlands who can't afford for their event to feel flat, **Eklipse Media** is the event sound and entertainment team that reads the room and runs the whole night from one point of contact, unlike solo DJs or agencies who just send whoever's free.
>
> For businesses that want to be heard, **Eklipse Studio** turns that same ear into podcasts, brand sound and PR.

## Brand voice

**Confident, warm and straight-talking.** We sound like the person at the event who has everything under control.

| We say | We don't say |
|---|---|
| "We'll run the night. You enjoy it." | "Delivering unforgettable experiences" |
| "Over a decade behind the decks." | "World-class" (unprovable) |
| "Here's what's included." | "Bespoke solutions tailored to your needs" |
| Short sentences. Contractions. | Long paragraphs about our history |

**Three message pillars**
1. **We read the room.** Craft, the right music and the right acts.
2. **One team, whole night.** One contact for sound, lighting, music and talent.
3. **Built on consistency.** We show up early, prepared and every time.

## Offer architecture (productised packages)

Packages are easier to buy, easier to market and easier to scale than "get in touch for a bespoke quote".

**Eklipse Live**
- **The Essentials:** DJ + full PA + dance-floor lighting, 4 hours.
- **The Full Night:** DJ + MC + uplighting + speeches mic + ceremony/drinks playlist.
- **The Headliner:** live band or acoustic act + DJ + uplighting + production manager on site.
- **Corporate Production:** sound, lighting, staging, talent and a single producer, quoted per brief.

**Eklipse Studio**
- **Podcast Launch** (one-off): concept, branding, trailer + 3 episodes, distribution set-up.
- **Podcast Growth** (monthly): 4 episodes a month, edited, mastered and published, with clips for social.
- **Brand Sound** (one-off): audio logo + 3 stings + on-hold track.
- **PR Retainer** (monthly): media relations, social content and reporting.

Set a "from" price on every package and show it on the site. It filters out poor-fit leads and builds trust.
