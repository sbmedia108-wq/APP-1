# Outreach scripts: venues, planners, corporate and Studio

## 1. Venues (email, then a visit)

**Subject:** Entertainment partner for [Venue name]?

Hi [Events manager name],

I'm [Name] from Eklipse Media. We're a new Warwickshire events team. I've been DJing since the 90s, and we now bring together a hand-picked roster of insured DJs and live acts for weddings and corporate events.

I'd love to be on your recommended supplier list. What we'd bring to [Venue]:
- **Punctual and tidy:** we arrive early, cable-manage everything and respect your noise limits and curfews.
- **One contact** for DJ, live acts and lighting, so your team only deals with us.
- **A perk for your couples/clients:** [free uplighting / 10% off] when they book through you.

Could I pop in for 15 minutes next week to say hello and see the space? Or we'd happily play a free showcase set at your next open evening.

Thanks,
[Name] · 01926 351664

**Follow-up after 5 days:** "Hi [Name], just bumping this up. I'll be near [town] on [day] and could drop in a one-pager. Would that suit?"

**Visit checklist:** the printed one-pager, a sample video on your phone, insurance and PAT certificates, and notes on the room's power, load-in and sound limiter.

## 2. Wedding planners

## 3. Corporate (LinkedIn, Feb–June)

**Connection request (≤300 characters):**
> Hi [Name], I run Eklipse Media, an events entertainment team in Warwickshire. We work with Midlands companies on Christmas parties, awards and launches. Would be good to connect.

**Follow-up message after they accept:**
> Thanks for connecting, [Name]. Quick one: has [Company] started thinking about the 2027 Christmas party yet? The best December Fridays go by early summer. We do sound, lighting, DJs and live acts as one package with one contact. Happy to send a couple of example set-ups if useful.

**Cold email version:**
**Subject:** [Company]'s Christmas party 2027

Hi [Name],

A slightly early question: are you organising [Company]'s Christmas party next year?

We're Eklipse Media, a Warwickshire events team. We handle DJs, live music, sound, lighting and staging as one package, so you only deal with one person and one invoice.

December Fridays usually book up by June. If it's on your list, I'll send a couple of example packages and prices. No hard sell.

[Name] · Eklipse Media

## 4. Studio: podcast pitch to local businesses

**Subject:** A podcast for [Company]?

Hi [Name],

I enjoyed [specific post/article from them]. You clearly have a lot to say about [their topic].

Have you thought about turning that into a podcast? We're Eklipse Media's studio team. We handle the concept, recording (at your office or ours), editing and publishing. You just show up and talk for 45 minutes a month, and we turn it into episodes plus short clips for LinkedIn.

Would a 15-minute chat be useful? I can show you what a first episode might look like.

[Name]

**Targets:** accountants, solicitors, estate agents, recruiters, coaches, B2B SaaS, local chambers of commerce, and venue owners. Anyone who already posts thought-leadership content on LinkedIn.
