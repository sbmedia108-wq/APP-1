# 30 days of social content (Instagram / TikTok / Facebook)

Post 3 times a week for 4 weeks (12 posts), plus the Stories ideas at the bottom. Every caption ends with the CTA. Use your own footage. Each post names the clip you need.

**Hashtag bank** (pick 5–8 per post, rotating):
`#weddingdjuk #warwickshirewedding #leamingtonspa #warwick #coventryevents #birminghamevents #midlandswedding #corporateevents #christmasparty #eventdj #livemusicuk #weddingentertainment #podcastproduction #ukwedding2027`

**Standard CTA:** *Build your quote in 2 minutes. Link in bio.*

---

### Week 1: introduce the new brand
**1. Reel: "We set the tone."**
Clip: a 7-second montage of your 5 best crowd moments, cut on the beat. Text on screen: "WE SET THE TONE."
> Over a decade behind the decks. Weddings, corporate nights, festivals, club nights, and every one read the room differently.
> New look, same ears. 🌘
> Build your quote in 2 minutes. Link in bio.

**2. Carousel: "What's actually in a DJ package?"**
Slides: 1) Title. 2) DJ + full PA. 3) Dance-floor lighting. 4) Speeches mic. 5) Ceremony and drinks playlist. 6) Set-up before guests arrive. 7) CTA.
> Most couples don't know what they're comparing when they get DJ quotes. Here's everything that comes as standard with ours. Save this for when you're shortlisting 📌

**3. Reel: set-up time-lapse**
Clip: an empty room through to a fully lit room in 10 seconds.
> From empty room to dance floor in 90 minutes. You'll never see this part, and that's the point.

### Week 2: proof and personality
**4. Reel: "POV: the first dance ends and the floor fills"**
> The moment we plan the whole night around. 💛 (Shared with the couple's permission.)

**5. Talking head: "3 things to ask any wedding DJ"**
Script: "1) Are you insured and PAT-tested? 2) Who actually turns up on the day? 3) What happens if your kit fails? If they hesitate on any of these, keep looking."
> Save this before you book anyone. Including us.

**6. Behind-the-scenes photo: the kit**
> Spare laptop. Spare mic. Spare cables. Spare everything. Reliability isn't luck. It's packing.

### Week 3: corporate and Studio
**7. Reel: corporate night montage**
> Awards, launches, Christmas parties. Sound, lighting, music and talent from one team, with one point of contact. Planning your 2027 Christmas party? The best dates go by June.

**8. Carousel: "Your brand has a look. What does it sound like?"** (Studio)
Slides: audio logo, on-hold music, event stings, podcast intro.
> Think of the sound when a Netflix show starts. That's audio branding. We make them for businesses of every size.

**9. Reel: podcast studio tour**
> Want to start a podcast in 2027? We handle the concept, recording, editing and publishing. You just turn up and talk.

### Week 4: urgency and conversion
**10. Static graphic: "2027 Saturdays are going."**
> Summer 2027 Saturdays are filling up. If you've got a date, check it now. It takes 2 minutes.

**11. Reel: genre switch**
Clip: 4 crowds and 4 genres (house, R&B, 90s, jazz trio), 2 seconds each.
> Deep house to Dancing Queen. Jazz trio to drum & bass. We play for the room you've got.

**12. Q&A Reel: answer a real question from DMs or comments**
> You asked: "Can you do the ceremony AND the party?" Yes. Acoustic act for the ceremony and drinks, then DJ for the evening, with one booking and no awkward changeover.
