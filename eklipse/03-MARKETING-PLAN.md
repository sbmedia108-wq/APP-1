# 03. Marketing plan: the first 90 days

> **Update:** With about 5 hours a week each, follow `05-ACTION-PLAN.md` and `06-BUDGET-AND-HOURS.md` first. This doc is the full menu. Don't start paid ads or wedding fairs until you have bookings and reviews.

**Goal for 90 days:** fill the 2027 wedding diary, win at least 5 corporate Christmas 2027 bookings, sign 2 venue partnerships and land the first Studio retainer.

**Budget assumption:** £500–£1,000/month in paid spend plus your time. Every tactic below is listed in order of return on effort. If money is tight, do the top five only.

## Timing matters: where we are on the calendar

It's late November 2026.
- **Now to early January is peak wedding-booking season.** Engagements spike over Christmas and New Year, and couples book suppliers in January and February. *This is the most valuable window of the year, and it's starting now.*
- **Corporate Christmas 2026 is mostly booked**, but last-minute and January "new year party" gaps exist. **Corporate Christmas 2027 gets booked from March to June**, so plan outreach for spring.
- **Q1 is quiet for live events**, which makes it the right time to sell Studio (podcasts launched for the new year).

## The channel plan (ranked)

### 1. Google Business Profile + reviews (free, highest return) 🔥
Most "wedding DJ near me" and "event entertainment Warwickshire" searches end in the map pack.
- Claim or verify the profile under Eklipse Media, category **DJ service**, secondary **Entertainment agency**, **Wedding service**, **Audio visual equipment supplier** and **Podcast production** (if available).
- Upload 20+ real photos, add every service with a "from" price, and post weekly (copy in `marketing/google-business-posts.md`).
- **Reviews system:** text a review link to every client the day after their event (script in `marketing/email-sequences.md`). **Target: 25 reviews in 90 days**, starting with past clients from the last 2 years.

### 2. Venue and planner partnerships (highest return per hour) 🔥
One venue's recommended-supplier list can be worth 10–30 bookings a year.
- Build a list of 40 venues within 45 minutes of Warwick (hotels, barns, country houses, golf clubs, conference centres).
- Send the outreach sequence in `marketing/outreach-scripts.md`, then visit in person with the one-pager.
- Offer venues a free showcase set at their next open evening or wedding fair, plus a referral fee or a guest discount.
- **Target: 2 signed venue partners in 90 days, 6 in 12 months.**

### 3. The new website + local SEO
- Launch the redesigned site (`site/`). The meta fixes on the current site are needed today regardless.
- Then add one page per service per area over time: `/wedding-dj-warwickshire`, `/corporate-event-entertainment-birmingham`, `/podcast-production-midlands` and so on. Each needs 600+ words, real photos, FAQs and a link to the quote builder.
- List the business on Hitched, Bridebook, Add to Event and Poptop. Use the same name, address and phone (NAP) everywhere.

### 4. Short-form video: Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts
Events are made for video. You already have the content, you just need to film it.
- **3 posts a week.** Formats: the crowd drop, a 15-second "set-up in time-lapse", first-dance moments (with permission), a "what a £X package looks like" walkthrough and the "behind the decks" POV.
- 30 days of captions are ready in `marketing/social-30-days.md`.
- Every post ends with the same CTA: "Build your quote, link in bio."

### 5. LinkedIn for corporate and Studio
- The founder posts twice a week: event lessons, behind-the-scenes from corporate jobs and podcast tips.
- Direct outreach to office managers, PAs, HR/people teams and event managers at Midlands companies with 50+ staff (script in `outreach-scripts.md`). Start in **February** for Christmas 2027.

### 6. Paid ads (only once 1–3 are live)
- **Google Search**, £15–25/day, tight keywords: "wedding dj warwickshire", "corporate event dj coventry", "christmas party entertainment birmingham", "podcast production midlands". Ad copy is in `marketing/ad-copy.md`.
- **Meta (Instagram/Facebook)**, £10/day, targeting people within 40 miles who are recently engaged, using your best video as the creative. Run it January to March.
- Track cost per enquiry. Kill anything over £40 per enquiry after 2 weeks.

### 7. Wedding fairs (selective)
Do 2–3 a year at venues you want to partner with, not big generic shows. Bring a compact demo set-up, a screen looping video, the QR code to the quote builder and a fair-only offer (for example, free uplighting if booked within 14 days).

### 8. Email (low effort, compounding)
- Every enquiry goes into a simple CRM and gets the nurture sequence (`marketing/email-sequences.md`).
- Send a quarterly newsletter to past clients and venues. Corporate clients rebook yearly if you remind them.

## The weekly marketing rhythm (about 5 hours a week)

| Day | Task | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Post to Google Business Profile, review the last week's numbers | 30 min |
| Tuesday | 5 venue or corporate outreach messages | 45 min |
| Wednesday | Edit and post a video from the weekend | 45 min |
| Thursday | LinkedIn post + 5 connection requests | 30 min |
| Friday | Chase reviews from the week's events, post a video | 30 min |
| Event days | Film 10 clips + 10 photos (assign a person to do this) | During the event |

## KPIs (tracked weekly)

| Metric | Now (estimated) | 90-day target |
|---|---|---|
| Google reviews | ~0 visible | 25+ |
| Website enquiries a month | unknown | 30+ |
| Enquiry → booking rate | unknown | 30%+ |
| Average booking value | unknown | +20% via packages |
| Venue partners | 0 | 2 |
| Studio retainers | 0? | 1–2 |
| Cost per enquiry (paid) | n/a | < £30 |

Put "How did you hear about us?" on every enquiry. Without it, none of this can be measured.
