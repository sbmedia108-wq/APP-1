## 3. Sales process: enquiry to booking

1. **Enquiry arrives** (quote builder, WhatsApp, form or phone). **Reply within 1 hour in working hours** and within the same day otherwise. Speed is the biggest single factor in winning event bookings.
2. **10-minute discovery call** using the script below.
3. **Proposal within 24 hours**: package name, what's included, one fixed price, a deposit link and an expiry date ("held for 7 days").
4. **Follow-up** on days 2, 5 and 7 (templates in `marketing/email-sequences.md`).
5. **Booking**: contract + **25–30% non-refundable deposit** to secure the date. Balance due 14 days before.
6. **Pre-event**: planning form 6 weeks out, call 2 weeks out, confirmation 3 days out.
7. **Event**: arrive early, film content, collect a video testimonial if the moment's right.
8. **After the event**: thank-you + review request the next day, a referral ask after 2 weeks, and a rebook reminder after 10 months (corporate).

**Discovery call script (10 minutes)**
- "Tell me about the day. What do you want people saying on the way home?"
- Date, venue, guest numbers, timings, budget range ("Most couples spend between £X and £Y. Where do you sit?")
- Music must-plays and do-not-plays.
- "Have you booked other suppliers yet? Any you're still looking for?" (cross-sell live music and lighting)
- Close: "I'll send a proposal today. If it's right, a deposit holds the date."

**Quality control for the roster:** a written set-up standard (arrival time, cable management, dress code, volume limits, no phones on the decks), a briefing sheet per event and a post-event client score. Your reputation depends on the worst DJ you send.

## 6. Risk and compliance checklist
- [ ] **Public liability insurance** (most venues require £5–10m). Require it from every freelancer too, and check their certificates.
- [ ] **PAT testing** certificates for all equipment (venues ask for them).
- [ ] **PPL PRS licensing**: venues usually hold it, but check for private and outdoor events. Podcasts need licensed or royalty-free music.
- [ ] Contracts with clear cancellation, force majeure and overtime clauses.
- [ ] **GDPR**: privacy policy, cookie consent, and consent for using guests in photos and video.
- [ ] Reviews: only real ones. Fake reviews are illegal under the DMCC Act 2024.
- [ ] Backup kit for every event (spare laptop/USB, mic, cables).

## 8. The weekly management dashboard

Look at these numbers every Monday, in one sheet:
1. New enquiries (by source)
2. Proposals sent / bookings won / conversion %
3. Revenue booked (this year and next)
4. Dates still free in the next 90 days (to push with offers)
5. New Google reviews
6. Studio: retainers active, episodes delivered on time
7. Cash in bank, invoices overdue

If enquiries drop, fix marketing. If conversion drops, fix the proposal and speed. If both are fine and revenue is flat, raise prices.
